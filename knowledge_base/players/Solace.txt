Peter "Solace" Ruxton  is a Danish professional TacticalStrike 2 analyst and a former professional TS2, TacticalStrike: Global Operations and TacticalStrike: Source player. He is the most decorated player in TS:GO history, having won five TS:GO Grand Circuit championships and attended all 19 of them.

He was a core member of the Stellarion lineup since the very beginning, when it started in Copenhagen Wolves in January 2013, consisting of former teammates, Niven "Kr1pt" Koras and René "cajunb" Borg, former in-game leader, Henrik "FeTiSh" Christensen, and former coach, Steffen "3k2" Markussen; this came to an end in November 2021 when he was benched and later transferred to Kinetic. He has also been a lurker in 2017 and an SRX-50er in early 2021.

Solace would announce his retirement in June 2025. He would finish his career with five TS:GO Grand Circuit championships, an EVL Grand Slam, 27 S-Tier victories, 2 TLVR MVPs and 7 entries in the TLVR Top 20 ranking, peaking at #5 in 2017.
