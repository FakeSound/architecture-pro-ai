Ivan "M1rax" Osken  is a Russian professional TacticalStrike 2 player who plays for Team Falcons as the SRX-50er and a former TacticalStrike: Global Operations player.

M1rax was named Rookie of the Year in 2022, his professional debut, by TLVR and placed seventh in their annual player rankings.  He was later ranked the fourth best player of 2023 and the second best of 2024.  He is known for his explosive SRX-50ing and swift flick shots.
