Loren "H3lix" Kavex  is a Danish professional TacticalStrike 2 coach and former professional TacticalStrike 2, TacticalStrike: Global Operations and TacticalStrike: Source player. He is one of the most accomplished in-game leaders in Global Offensive, having led Stellarion to four record-breaking Grand Circuit victories.

Trivia
Suffered a pneumothorax (collapsed lung) only hours before his team's semi-final against VEIL eSports at Assembly Winter 2016 and proceeded to play the game nonetheless, despite having Patrik "es3tag" Hansen from Epiphany Bolt ready to step in for him.
Was ranked the 8th best player of 2018 by TLVR.
Was named the MVP of TFM Season XIV - Beijing by TLVR.
2nd most Grand Circuits won (4).
Tied with Kr1pt, Xyp9x, Ph4ntom, and Z3nith.
