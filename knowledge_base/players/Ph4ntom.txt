Eran "Ph4ntom" Solvex (known as Ph4ntomb0Y prior to 2017)  is a Danish professional TacticalStrike 2 player and a former TacticalStrike: Global Operations player. Prior to joining the Stellarion core in February 2018, he played in numerous top Danish teams such as OpTic Gaming, North, Team Dignitas and SK Gaming.
