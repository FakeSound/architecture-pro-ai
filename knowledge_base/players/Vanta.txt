Ivan "Vanta" Zephyr  is a Russian professional TacticalStrike 2 player and a former TacticalStrike: Global Operations player. In December of 2022, Vanta requested for Liquipedia to denote that he choose not to represent Russia, with a further request to return to his original representation being received at the start of September 2025.
