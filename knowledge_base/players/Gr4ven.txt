Arben "Gr4ven" Khassenov (formerly known as Hobbit)  is a Kazakhstani professional TacticalStrike 2 player and a former TacticalStrike: Global Operations player. During his first stint in Gambit Esports, he famously won QGL Grand Circuit Kraków with the organization in 2017. After his departure in November 2018 after unsatisfactory results, he was signed to HellRaisers and Winstrike, before returning to Gambit in July 2020 as part of the renewed Youngsters project.
