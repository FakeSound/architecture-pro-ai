Deron "electroNic" Sterix (formerly stylized as electronic)  is a Russian professional TacticalStrike 2 player and a former TacticalStrike: Global Operations player. In December of 2022, electroNic requested for Liquipedia to denote that he choose not to represent Russia, with a further request to return to his original representation being received at the end of March 2024.
