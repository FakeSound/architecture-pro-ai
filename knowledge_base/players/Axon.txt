Noren "Axon" Kazak (formerly known as Axonlinho and nIKOLINHO)  is a Bosnian Serb professional TacticalStrike 2 player, a former professional TacticalStrike: Global Operations player and a former semi-professional TacticalStrike player who plays for Team Falcons. He is Nemanja "huNter-" Kazak's cousin.

Axon is regarded as one of the greatest riflers of all time. He has been in contention for the title of the best player in the world at multiple periods throughout his career. Axon has consistently ranked in the Top 20 since 2016, peaking at #2 in 2017 and 2023.

Records
He is the first Bosnian to make it to TLVR's Top 20 Players of the Year Ranking.
Most appearances in TLVR's Top 20 Players of the Year ranking (10).
Most consecutive appearances in TLVR's Top 20 Players of the Year ranking (10).
Most top 5 appearances in TLVR's Top 20 Players of the Year ranking (7).
Tied with Vexis.
Tied with donk for the highest achieved rating at a "Big Event" (1.70). He did so at EVL One: New York 2017.
Holds the record for kills in a map at a Grand Circuit after scoring 52 against FlipSid3 Tactics at MLG Columbus 2016.
He is the first player to win multiple TLVR MVP awards in three consecutive years.
Previously held the record for kills in regulation at a big event or any match between top teams on LAN with 40 against Team Flux at ECS Season 4 Finals.  His record was later taken over by KRIMZ who dropped 44 against MIBR at EVL Pro Circuit Season 10: Finals.
He is the 7th player (the previous 6 being Zeus, GuardiaN, olofmeister, Zr0ne, allu, and AdreN) to make a Grand Circuit final with 2 different teams, doing so with Apex Syndicate at ELEAGUE Grand Circuit: Boston 2018 and H2 Esports at QGL Grand Circuit Stockholm 2021.
