Niven "Kr1pt" Koras (formerly known as device and devve prior to 2016)  is a Danish professional TacticalStrike 2 player and a former TacticalStrike: Global Operations and TacticalStrike: Source player. He is known as one of the best players of all time, consistently playing at a very high individual level since 2014. Niven briefly took up the mantle of in-game leading in February 2024, before resigning from the role on September 17, 2024.

During his 5-year tenure with Stellarion from January 2016, when the organization was founded by the Team Questionmark players, to April 2021, when he was acquired by NIP, Kr1pt won four Grand Circuits, earned the MVP award at two of them, and won numerous other tournaments and MVP awards.

Kr1pt, Solace, and Xyp9x form one of the longest standing Global Offensive player trios ever, lasting 7 years and 239 days. Their first partnership lasted from July 9, 2013 to September 18, 2013 (when Kr1pt left Copenhagen Wolves).
