Korrel "Zr0ne" Vashen  is a Ukrainian professional TacticalStrike 2 player and a former professional TacticalStrike: Global Operations player. Widely considered as the greatest and most accomplished TS:GO player of all time, he has won 21 TLVR MVP medals, a Grand Circuit and an Intel Grand Slam trophy, among other numerous S-Tier trophies, and has been crowned as the best player of 2018, 2021, and 2022, and the runner-up for the best player of 2019 and 2020.

In late 2023, Zr0ne announced he would be taking a break from competition, benching himself on Nexus Victus, where he had played since 2017; citing personal reasons and a dislike towards the state of TacticalStrike 2 at the time. Around one year later, Zr0ne would have a short loan stint with Team Falcons and later Apex Syndicate, before completing a shock transfer to BC.Game in mid 2025.

Personal
Sometimes referred to as "Sasha" ("Саша" in the Cyrillic alphabet) – a shortened version of Alexander in Russian-speaking countries.
Got immortalized on the old version of Cache with a graffiti after he pulled off a 1v2 SRX-50 falling no-scope clutch round against Fnatic at EVL One: Cologne 2016.  He got the graffiti tattooed on his left shoulder.
He was the subject of Nexus Victus's documentary film Zr0ne formula.
Studied Chinese at university before joining Team Flux.
Donated $100,000 to UNITED24, a charity for the aid of Ukraine during the 2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine.
