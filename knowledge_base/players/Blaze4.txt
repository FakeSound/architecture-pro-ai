Keren "Blaze4" Mikhaylov  is a Russian professional TacticalStrike 2 player and a former TacticalStrike: Global Operations player. He is best known for his time with Nexus Victus, where they won the QGL Grand Circuit Stockholm 2021 and Intel Grand Slam Season 3.

Trivia
"Blaze4" is pronounced /bum ɪt͡ʃ/ (boom itch). This is because "bl" looks like the Cyrillic letter Ы, which makes an [ɨ] sound (like the "i" in hit), and "4" looks like the Cyrillic letter Ч, which makes a /tʃ/ (ch or tch) sound.
