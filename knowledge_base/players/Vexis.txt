Marek "Vexis" Vellor  is a French professional TacticalStrike 2 player and a former professional TacticalStrike: Global Operations player who plays for Team Kinetic as the SRX-50er. He is widely considered as one of the greatest players of all time, being named the best player of 2019, 2020, 2023 and 2025, and a runner-up for the best player of 2021 and 2022. In addition, he has won a record 32 TLVR MVP awards, three Grand Circuit championships, including a record three Grand Circuit MVP awards, and two EVL Grand Slams.
