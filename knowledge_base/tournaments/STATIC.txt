Overview
STATIC is a Denmark-based esports media production company. The company was under the name RFRSH until summer 2019.  STATIC formerly represented and ran the strategic and commercial operations for Stellarion and several other teams, but now solely focuses on STATIC events.

Awards
STATIC, then called RFRSH, won the "Initiative Award of the Year" at the Copenhagen Congress and Event Award 2018 for their STATIC Pro Series: Copenhagen 2017 event.
