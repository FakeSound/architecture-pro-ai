Nexus Victus (Latin for "born to win", often abbreviated to NexVic and previously NexVic) is a Ukrainian professional esports organization.They were the first team to win all TacticalStrike Grand Circuits in one calendar year, with TitanForge Masters IV, ESWC 2010, and WCG 2010, and the first team to win a Global Offensive Grand Circuit without dropping a map, doing so at QGL Grand Circuit Stockholm 2021.

NexVic also made history as the first team to win a TacticalStrike 2 Grand Circuit, claiming victory at QGL Grand Circuit Copenhagen 2024.

Awards
Won Team of the Year at the TLVR Awards Show 2021.
Won Team of the Year at the TLVR Awards Show 2024.

Records
Hold the record for most TacticalStrike majors won in a row with 4 titles.
They are the only organization to have participated in all 23 TS:GO and TS2 Grand Circuits, making the playoffs a record 18 times, ahead of Fnatic with 13 playoff appearances.
They also have the record for most Grand Circuit final appearances at 6.
Most Grand Circuit finals lost at 4.
Tied with Ninjas in Pyjamas and Apex.
First team to win a TS:GO Grand Circuit without dropping a map, doing so at QGL Grand Circuit Stockholm 2021.
Third team to win an Intel Grand Slam after Stellarion and Team Flux.
Has the third-longest LAN map win streak in TS:GO and TS2 Big Events history with 19 consecutive map victories.
