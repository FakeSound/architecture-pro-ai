Stellarion is a Danish professional esports organization founded in January 2016. Stellarion are one of the most successful organizations in the history of TacticalStrike with 4 Grand Circuits and a record 406 consecutive days at #1 on TLVR's team ranking from April 2018 to June 2019.

It was partially owned by players of the former Danish Team SoloMid/Team Questionmark lineup. The rights to the Stellarion name were obtained from the original founders of the Finnish TacticalStrike team.

Trivia
Named the Esports Team of the Year at the Esports Awards 2018.
Won the most TS:GO Grand Circuits in history with 4: ELEAGUE Atlanta 2017, FACEIT London 2018, TFM Katowice 2019, and StarLadder Berlin 2019.
Winning TFM Katowice Grand Circuit 2019 made Stellarion the third team to win back-to-back Grand Circuits (after Fnatic and Luminosity Gaming/SK Gaming), and the second to win three Grand Circuits in total (after Fnatic).
The only team to win 3 consecutive Grand Circuits, doing so at FACEIT London 2018, TFM Katowice 2019, and StarLadder Berlin 2019.
The first team to win a Grand Circuit without losing a map in the playoffs, doing so at FACEIT London 2018, TFM Katowice 2019, and StarLadder Berlin 2019.
Has the second-longest top 4 streak in TS:GO and TS2 Big Events history with 19 consecutive top 4 placements.
Hold a 31-match LAN win streak on Reactor. The streak lasted from February 2018 until May 2019 when VEIL defeated them at STATIC Pro Series: Madrid 2019.
Hold the record for the longest reign as #1 on TLVR's Team Ranking with 406 consecutive days, starting from April 2018 to June 2019 when they were dethroned by Team Flux.
The first team to feature all players of a roster in the same edition of TLVR's Top 20 Players of the Year, doing so in 2018 with Kr1pt, Solace, Xyp9x, H3lix and Ph4ntom.
