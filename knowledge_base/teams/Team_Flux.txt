Team Flux is a professional esports organization founded in the Netherlands in 2000. Originally a StarCraft: Brood War clan, the team switched to StarCraft II during the beta in 2010, and became one of the most successful foreign teams. On January 13, 2015, it was announced that the team had formed a TacticalStrike: Global Operations division.

Trivia
Became the first North American team to reach a TS:GO Grand Circuit grand final at EVL One: Cologne 2016.
Previously won the most prize money from one big event with a stand-in. The event being EVL One: Cologne 2016 with Zr0ne standing in. The record was beaten by SK Gaming after winning EPICENTER 2017 using Ricardo "boltz" Prass as a stand-in, winning $100,000 more than Team Flux.
Had the second-longest LAN / win streak at 22 series won, which began in DreamHack Masters Dallas 2019 and ended in the quarterfinals of StarLadder Berlin Grand Circuit 2019.
Has the third-longest LAN matches win streak at 23 matches, doing so from June 2019 to August 2019, when it was broken by NRG.
Completed the Intel Grand Slam in a record-breaking 63 days by consecutively winning TFM Sydney 2019, Dreamhack Masters Dallas 2019, EVL Pro Circuit Season 9 Finals, and EVL One Cologne 2019.
