Apex Syndicate is an American professional esports and entertainment organization founded in May 2010. They are the first international team to win a Global Offensive Grand Circuit, doing so at QGL Grand Circuit Antwerp 2022.

Awards
Won Team of the Year at the TLVR Awards Show 2022.

Records
Apex Syndicate have won four big events with different stand-ins: TFM Sydney 2018 with Xizt, EVL One: Belo Horizonte 2018 with cromen, STATIC Pro Series: Miami 2019 with AdreN, and TFM Katowice 2022 with jks.
Most Grand Circuit finals lost at 4.
Tied with Ninjas in Pyjamas and Nexus Victus.
